ABOUT INAMIGOS

Building a Better Future Together

InAmigos Foundation is a Section 8 registered non-profit organization founded on September 23, 2020. The foundation works towards community development and social empowerment through impactful and sustainable initiatives.

Its work focuses on education, women empowerment, animal welfare, environmental sustainability, food distribution, social inclusion and skill development.
