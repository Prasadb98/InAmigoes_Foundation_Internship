About Our NGO

Hope Hands is a community-based NGO started with a simple goal: to help people who need support and create better opportunities for everyone.

We work with volunteers and local communities on education, food distribution, cleanliness and other social activities.

Our Ongoing Project

Education For Every Child

Many children do not have access to proper educational resources. Our current project provides books, school supplies and basic learning support to children from underprivileged families.

Our volunteers regularly visit local communities and organize learning activities for children.
